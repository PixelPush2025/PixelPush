# Pixel Push Money Dashboard

A dashboard for tracking your platform subscriptions, client retainers, payments and profit.

## Open it
Open `dashboard/index.html` in any browser. No install or server is needed.
To try it out, go to **Settings → Load sample data**.

## What it does
- **Overview**: monthly recurring revenue, subscription costs, expected and actual profit, year-to-date profit, a 12-month chart, what's due in the next 30 days, and breakdowns by category and client.
- **Subscriptions**: the platforms you pay for (Canva, Adobe, Meta, hosting and so on), with billing cycle, next due date and status. Click **Mark paid** to log the payment and move the due date forward.
- **Clients**: recurring client retainers. Click **Mark received** to log the income.
- **Payments**: the full ledger, including one-off payments, with filters by type, month and search.
- **Settings**: currency, JSON backup and restore, and CSV export.

## Where data lives
Your data is saved in this browser's local storage, so it stays on your device only. Use **Export backup** regularly, and **Import backup** to move your data to another device or browser.
